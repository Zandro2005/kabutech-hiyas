Master Hardware Wiring & Pinout Specification

This document is the official engineering standard for the Kabutech Hiyas automated mushroom and crop cultivation chamber. It specifies the electrical interfaces, pin assignments, communication protocols, cable assemblies, and protective circuits connecting the central ESP32-S3 controller to the TCA9548A I2C multiplexer, 3.2" SPI TFT display, three 10-meter GX16 aviation sensor umbilicals, and opto-isolated 12V actuator relays.

Architectural Upgrade — TCA9548A Hardware Bus Multiplexing: Splicing three 10-meter aviation cables in star topology stacks lumped capacitance to $pprox 3,600 ext{ pF}$, exceeding the $400 ext{ pF}$ I2C standard by 900% and causing bus lockup. Integrating a single TCA9548A multiplexer (~₱100) inside the main controller box isolates each cable to its own sub-bus ($pprox 1,200 ext{ pF}$), guaranteeing zero signal reflection, zero cross-channel noise, and rock-solid telemetry across the BH1750, SHT31, and SCD41 probes.

TCA9548A Multiplexer & 10-Meter Sensor Wiring

To eliminate bus capacitance breakdown and cross-talk, three dedicated GX16 aviation ports connect to Channels 0, 1, and 2 of the TCA9548A module. The ESP32 switches channels sequentially in firmware before reading each sensor.

3. Software Channel Selection & Non-Blocking Polling Protocol

#define TCA9548A_ADDR 0x70

void tcaSelect(uint8_t channel) {
  if (channel > 7) return;
  Wire.beginTransmission(TCA9548A_ADDR);
  Wire.write(1 << channel);   // Writes 0x01 (CH0), 0x02 (CH1), or 0x04 (CH2)
  Wire.endTransmission();
}

void tcaDisableAll() {
  Wire.beginTransmission(TCA9548A_ADDR);
  Wire.write(0x00);          // Electrically disconnects all 3x 10m cables
  Wire.endTransmission();
}
Cable Shield Ground-Loop Warning: The braided shield of each 10-meter aviation cable must be connected to System GND only at the main controller enclosure. Leave the shield completely disconnected (floating) at the sensor probe inside the chamber to avoid ground loops that inject 60Hz hum into the I2C clock.

3.2" SPI TFT Display (ILI9341) Pinout & Interconnect

The local cabinet screen is a 3.2-inch 240×320 SPI TFT display with an ILI9341 controller. It is connected to dedicated high-speed GPIOs on the ESP32-S3, avoiding conflicts with actuator relay lines (GPIO 9, 11, 12, 13) and I2C lines (GPIO 4, 5).

CRITICAL HARDWARE RULE (JUMPER J1 ON BACK OF TFT):
The display module is powered from 5V / VIN. The copper solder jumper labeled J1 on the back of the red display PCB must remain COMPLETELY OPEN (UNSHORTED). Feeding 5V into VCC with J1 bridged bypasses the onboard 3.3V LDO regulator and permanently destroys the ILI9341 panel!

Actuator Relays & Inductive Snubber Circuitry

The actuator subcircuit controls four heavy-duty environmental actuators inside the mushroom chamber using opto-isolated Songle SRD-05VDC-SL-C relays.

CRITICAL RELAY JUMPER SETTING (GALVANIC ISOLATION):
Remove the blue shunt jumper linking VCC and JD-VCC on the relay board! Connect JD-VCC to the LM2596 +5.0V output (to power coil electromagnets). Connect VCC to the ESP32 +3.3V rail (to power optocoupler LEDs). This completely isolates the ESP32 from inductive kickback.

2. Inductive Kickback Snubber Schematic & Installation

1N4007 Flyback Diode Orientation Rules Safety Critical

When an inductive load (fan motor, solenoid valve, high-pressure pump) is switched OFF by the relay, the collapsing magnetic field generates a reverse voltage spike up to 150V–200V.

A 1N4007 diode must be soldered directly across the load terminals in reverse parallel:

  • Cathode (Silver Band end): Connect to the +12V DC supply line.
  • Anode (Black end): Connect to the switched ground / return lead.

WARNING: If the diode is soldered backward, turning ON the relay causes a dead short across the 12V supply and will instantly destroy the diode!

2. Field Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Quick Guide

Symptom: Sensor Reads FAULT / NACK Troubleshooting

1. Check TCA9548A Address: Ensure pins A0, A1, A2 are connected to GND (address 0x70).
2. Check Sensor Channel: Confirm BH1750 is on CH0, SHT31 on CH1, SCD41 on CH2.
3. SCD41 Power Dip: Ensure the 100µF decoupling capacitor is soldered directly across SCD41 VDD and GND at the chamber end.
4. Bus Clock: Ensure Wire.setClock(50000) is active in firmware.

Symptom: TFT White Screen / Blank Troubleshooting

1. Check Backlight Pin: Ensure GPIO 2 is set to HIGH or outputting PWM (or test tied to 3.3V).
2. Check J1 Jumper: Must be OPEN when fed from 5V.
3. Check Reset Pin: Ensure GPIO 6 is connected to RESET.
4. Check SPI Clock: Ensure SCK is on GPIO 18 and MOSI is on GPIO 17 with wires < 15cm.
