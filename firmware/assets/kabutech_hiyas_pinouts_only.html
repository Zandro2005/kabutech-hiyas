5. Sensor Breakout Board Pinouts (Chamber End)
Sensor Breakout I2C Address Breakout Pins Wiring Instructions

Sensirion SHT31 (T/RH) 0x44 VIN, GND, SDA, SCL, ADR VIN→Pin 1, GND→Pin 2, SDA→Pin 3, SCL→Pin 4. ADR tied to GND.

SCD41 10-Meter Buffer Capacitor: Solder a 100µF 10V electrolytic capacitor + 0.1µF ceramic capacitor directly across Pin 1 (VDD) and Pin 2 (GND) at the SCD41 breakout board inside the chamber probe.
